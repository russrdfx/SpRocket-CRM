# Brand guide — recreated edition

## Proposed color palette
| Color | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Rocket blue | #1677E8 | Sprocket, highlighted R, brand accents |
| Exhaust orange | #FF8A24 | Exhaust and restrained highlights |
| Deep navy | #142B49 | Wordmark, primary text, dark surfaces |
| White | #FFFFFF | Light backgrounds and dark-theme text |
| Pale surface | #F4F7FB | CRM page backgrounds |

These are proposed recreation values, not sampled from the unavailable generated board. Keep orange primarily decorative; assess contrast separately for text and buttons in the CRM.

## Typography
The recreated wordmark uses Arial Bold with a blue capital R. CRM is smaller Arial Bold with generous letter spacing. Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif is the recommended implementation stack for this edition, chosen for broad availability. Use 400 for body copy, 700 for headings, and 14–16 px for normal UI text. Fonts are not bundled; their licenses remain with their owners. This is not an identification of the original generated font.

## Usage
Maintain aspect ratio and leave clear space equal to one sprocket tooth height on every side. Suggested minimum horizontal width: 180 px; stacked width: 140 px; standalone symbol: 32 px. Use the supplied favicon below 32 px and visually check it in the target browser. Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines or rotate the full mark. Both app tiles retain a white rocket. The mono versions use one ink with transparent rocket negative space.
